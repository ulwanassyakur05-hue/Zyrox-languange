What works in V3.3?

The playground currently executes the documented display statements:

  • Print.true("text") — displays the text.
  • Print.false("text") — suppresses the text.
  • Blank lines and // comments are ignored.

CORE vs PROPOSED

Only syntax with a defined behavior is listed as CORE. Concepts without a confirmed, complete grammar remain PROPOSED or reserved until the project owner approves their rules.

V3.3 is a browser demo, not a claim that every planned Zyrox feature is implemented.
